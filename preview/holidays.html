Holiday wardrobe

Seasonal outfits for the crew's crabs, drawn by the mod's own crab code at the strip's real size and three times larger. . They switch on by themselves in each holiday's window. To retire one, tick Drop and copy the list back to Claude.

3× stage

How they switch on

Outside every window the crabs wear their usual costumes. Moving holidays (Lunar New Year, Easter, Hanukkah, Diwali, Thanksgiving) are worked out or looked up per year; the bars below come from the same window code.

When

Each holiday has a date window. Where two overlap (Hanukkah inside December, Día de los Muertos at the end of October) their outfits share one pool.

Who wears what

  • Stable per session: a hash of the session id and the year picks the outfit, so a crab looks the same in every panel and after a restart, with nothing stored.
  • No twins: sessions take theirs in join order and a clash moves to the next free outfit.
  • Your faces rules and /crew face still win, and can name a holiday outfit (nut-*=witch).

Turning it off, or forcing one

The holidays option, plus a command for trying them out:

"pluginConfigs": {
  "session-crew": {
    "options": {
      "holidays": "auto",
      "skipHolidays": ["thanksgiving"]
    }
  }
}
  • auto (default) follows the calendar; off never dresses up; a holiday id (halloween) forces it whatever the date.
  • skipHolidays leaves out ones you don't celebrate.
  • /crew holiday <id|auto|off> overrides it until you change it back, kept like /crew strip.
